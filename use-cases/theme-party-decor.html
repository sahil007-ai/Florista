Theme Party Decor

Wholesale Flowers for Theme Party & Kids Birthday Decor

Butterflies, fish, angel wings and pastel florals for themed kids events.

Themed kids events need props more than backdrops — butterflies for a butterfly-themed first birthday, fish for an under-the-sea party, angel wings for a christening photo wall. Florista’s theme range was designed exactly for these moments.

Pair the themed props with soft pastel organza florals for the frame around the cake or the photo backdrop. The 12 inch and 18 inch organza work well as filler at kids-event scale — the 60 inch range is generally too imposing here.
