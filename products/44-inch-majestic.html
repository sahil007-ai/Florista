How buyers use the 44" Majestic

Pairs well with

Two 44" Majestics carry an entire stage. Most buyers go single-piece-bold — flanked by 24"/28" stepping outward — rather than ordering three or more.

Eight-layer petal construction means each flower takes about 90 minutes on the floor. We schedule these on the same day to keep colour batching consistent within an order — small detail, big visual difference.
