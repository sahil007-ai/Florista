Behind this piece

How buyers use the Cinderella Flowers

Pairs well with

Cinderella works in clusters — five or six close together rather than spread thin. Layer with a few 18" Lumora in matching shades for a soft halo effect around the focal point.

Behind the craft

The pearl centre is hand-set with a tweezers, not glued from a tube. We use 3 mm faux pearls — large enough to catch light, small enough not to read costume.

The Florista promise

Direct from our Nagpur factory floor — no middlemen, no inflated retail markup. Every flower is hand-cut, hand-pleated, and hand-finished by the same artisan team that has been making decor since 2018. Bulk pricing, honest MOQs, PAN India shipping.

Designing a fairytale moment?

Send the moodboard and the photographer's lighting style — we'll match the pearl tone to either film-warm or digital-cool, depending on what the camera will favour on the day.
