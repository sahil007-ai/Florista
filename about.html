Our Story

Florista was founded in Nagpur with one focused goal — to give Indian event decorators direct factory access to premium organza and fabric flowers, without the markup of resellers and middlemen.

We are a manufacturer, not a trader. Every flower in our catalogue is designed, sampled, and handcrafted in-house by our team of skilled artisans. From the 12-inch Ornela to the 60-inch Giant Flora, each piece is built to be reused across dozens of events while holding its shape and colour.

Today we supply wedding decorators, event-management firms, stage contractors, and retail outlets across PAN India — from our Nagpur production base. Our pricing stays wholesale-direct because we work directly with the artisans who make every piece.

  • Direct from factory — no middleman pricing
  • Own R&D — creating new designs regularly
  • Durable, washable, and foldable for safe transport
  • Consistent bulk supply with reliable lead times
