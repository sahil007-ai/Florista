How buyers use the Aura Flower (3ft)

Pairs well with

The Aura works with negative space, not layered around. Place five or six in deliberate intervals across a wide backdrop — resist the impulse to fill the gaps with smaller sizes.

Behind the craft

The petal angle is set during a single hand-press so the structural form stays consistent across an order. Frees a full day on the production floor for batch — which is why we hold these to a 5-piece MOQ.
