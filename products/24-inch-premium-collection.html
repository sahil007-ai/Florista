Behind this piece

How buyers use the 24" Premium Collection

Pairs well with

Most premium bookings combine 8 – 12 of these with 1 – 2 of our 36"/40" focal pieces for a layered hero wall. A 60/40 mix of Premium and Wedding Touch hits the right price/finish balance.

Behind the craft

Sheer organza printed in small runs. We cap each shade at 80 pieces a month to keep the print register clean — if a buyer needs more in the same shade, we schedule a fresh print pass instead of stretching the run.

The Florista promise

Direct from our Nagpur factory floor — no middlemen, no inflated retail markup. Every flower is hand-cut, hand-pleated, and hand-finished by the same artisan team that has been making decor since 2018. Bulk pricing, honest MOQs, PAN India shipping.
