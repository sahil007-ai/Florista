How buyers use the 36" Premium Blooms

Pairs well with

One 36" anchors a panel; three across a 16-foot backdrop reads symmetric without crowding. Most buyers pair with matching 18" – 24" in the same palette to layer outward.

Behind the craft

The 36" frame is welded, not bent — at this size, hand-bent wire flexes during shipping. Welded joints add 8 minutes per unit but eliminate post-transit reshape work at the venue.
